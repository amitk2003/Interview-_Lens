1. Fix: Google Meet URL Opening Bug
Why did localhost:3000/meet.google.com/... happen?
When a meeting URL like meet.google.com/izk-cpnu-jys was entered without https://, JavaScript's window.open() treated it as a relative website path instead of an external website, causing the browser to navigate to localhost:3000/meet.google.com/....

What was updated in 

CreateInterviewModal.jsx
:
Automatic Protocol Normalization: Any URL entered without https:// is prepended with https://.
Instant Google Meet Shortcut: Added a + Create Instant Google Meet Call one-click button that launches https://meet.google.com/new in a new tab.
2. Deep Technical Research: Automated Google Meet Capture
A common question is: Can a web app automatically join and transcribe a Google Meet call just from a URL?

🔒 Browser Security Constraint
Because of Same-Origin Policy and WebRTC security isolation, a website in one tab (localhost:3000) cannot silently tap into the microphone or audio stream of another tab (meet.google.com) without explicit user permission or an authorized bridge.

Option A: Headless Meeting Bot (e.g., Recall.ai API / Puppeteer Bot)
When you enter a Google Meet URL, the backend sends a bot participant (e.g., "InterviewLens Capture Bot") to join the call.
The bot records the audio stream, runs real-time speech-to-text (Whisper/Deepgram), and streams chunks into InterviewLens.
Option B: Chrome Extension / Tab Audio Capture
A lightweight browser extension with chrome.tabCapture captures the Google Meet audio and live closed-caption DOM directly on your machine.
Option C: InterviewLens Built-in Interactive Live AI Room (Ready to use)
If you want an end-to-end simulated mock interview, select the Interactive AI Interviewer Room option in InterviewLens. It asks role-specific questions with real-time voice speech recognition and analyzes your answers directly.
Option D: Transcript / Recording Upload (Post-Meeting)
Export Google Meet's transcript or recording (.vtt, .srt, .txt, .mp3, .wav) and upload it to run the multi-agent evaluation.
