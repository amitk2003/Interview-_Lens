Part 2: Your Best AI Project — InterviewLens (90–120 Seconds)
Goal: Clearly address the 4 required points: Problem $\to$ Approach $\to$ Tech Stack $\to$ Challenges Overcome.

1. The Problem
"Most automated technical interview and evaluation tools fail for two reasons: pure AI interview bots hallucinate, penalizing candidates for things they never said, while pure human interview panels take days, cost thousands, and burn senior engineering hours. I wanted to build a platform that gives hiring teams the speed of AI with the credibility and nuance of verified human experts."

2. Approach & Your Specific Contributions
"I designed and built InterviewLens, a full-stack technical interview evaluation platform with a Human-in-the-Loop review workflow:

Asynchronous Multi-Stage Analysis: Using asyncio.gather, I orchestrated parallel agents that ingest interview transcripts and evaluate technical depth, problem-solving, and communication across 7 core competencies.
Algorithmic Fact-Checking: I built a deterministic Verification Agent that validates every AI critique against verbatim transcript timestamps. If the candidate never actually mentioned a concept, the claim is rejected and its score impact is zeroed out.
Human-in-the-Loop Calibration: I engineered an expert assignment and review console where vetted industry experts review the timestamped evidence, adjust scores with written justifications, and sign off before reports are released to candidates."*
3. Technologies, Tools & Frameworks
"On the backend, I used Python 3.12 and FastAPI with Pydantic v2 for strict schema validation, integrated with Groq Llama 3.3 70B for sub-second agentic inference and an OpenAI fallback. Data is persisted in MongoDB Atlas with a local JSON fallback. The frontend is built in React 18 and Vite, deployed on Vercel, with the backend containerized on Render."

4. Key Challenges & How You Overcame Them
"The biggest engineering hurdle was security and authorization integrity:

First, I discovered an IDOR vulnerability where authenticated candidates could request reviews on other candidates' assessments, and OAuth sign-in was usurping assessments. I solved this by implementing strict server-side resource ownership checks verifying the candidate's email before any review action.
Second, I solved a conflict-of-interest loophole where an expert could claim their own interview assessment by building an automated blocker that rejects self-claims with 409 Conflict.
Finally, I built an append-only security audit logger with recursive PII and token redaction, and backed the entire platform with 51 automated integration tests with 100% pass rate."*
